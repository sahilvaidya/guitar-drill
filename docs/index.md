# Guitar Drill — Support

Guitar Drill is a fretboard trainer for iPhone: learn the notes on the neck,
train your ear, and study chords, triads and scales. It works fully offline.

## Get help

Questions, bug reports or feature ideas? Email
[guitardrillsupport@gmail.com](mailto:guitardrillsupport@gmail.com).
Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when
the problem happened.

## Common questions

**Does the app need an internet connection?**
No. Everything works offline, and nothing is sent anywhere.

**How do I reset my stats?**
Open Settings and tap *Reset all stats*. Your settings (mode, fret range) are kept.

**There's no sound in the Ear tab.**
Check that your volume is up and try replaying the prompt. Drill audio plays
even when the iPhone is on silent, but not if the volume is at zero.

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
