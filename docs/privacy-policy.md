# Guitar Drill — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-29_

Guitar Drill does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

**What the app stores.** Your practice stats, recent misses, best scores and
settings (practice mode, fret range, ear-training options) are saved on your
device only, so they survive relaunching the app. Ear-training audio is
generated on your device and cached temporarily in the app's cache folder.

**What the app does not do.** It has no accounts or login, makes no network
requests, includes no analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not access
your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

**Deleting your data.** Use *Settings → Reset all stats* to clear your stats,
or delete the app to remove everything it has stored.

**Children.** The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
